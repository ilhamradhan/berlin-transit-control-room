System documentation

Definitions and boundaries for the public evidence product.

What this shows

Compact mode, route, and day aggregates generated from a verified release. It is a static, read-only artifact; it does not represent live service state.

Limitations

Predictions are not actual arrivals. The finite campaign is not causal evidence, and no weather or disruption attribution is inferred. Low coverage qualifies every affected view.

Metric definitions

Median delay
Middle predicted delay across observations.
P90 delay
Predicted delay at the 90th percentile.
On time
Predicted arrival no more than five minutes late.
Severe delay
Predicted arrival more than fifteen minutes late.
Cancellation
Source-provided cancellation rate; unavailable when the release has no cancellation field.
Realtime coverage
Share of expected collection slots represented by the verified release.
Schedule match
Share of observations matched to a validated static schedule.

Source contracts

Aggregates come from a verified, read-only release of the accepted finite campaign. Raw payloads, event rows, trip identifiers, private paths, and secrets never enter the public artifact.

Architecture

Collection and transformation remain private. The public product reads one compact artifact; local retrieval is citation-preserving and read-only. No live endpoint, provider, or operational control is exposed.

Runbook guidance

Check release identity, generated time, coverage, and the limitations above before interpreting a view. Treat stale or low-coverage artifacts as qualified evidence; do not infer causes or current service state.
